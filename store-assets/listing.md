## Detailed description

TabView turns your overflowing tab bar into a clean visual grid. Open it and every tab becomes a live preview card, so you can finally see what you actually have open across all your windows.

What you can do:

• See all tabs at once as screenshot previews, no clicking through them one by one
• Track memory: each card shows its memory use, and heavy tabs get flagged so you know what to close
• Group tabs into Chrome tab groups with a name and color
• Merge every window into one with a single click, groups preserved
• Discard tabs to free memory without losing your place
• Search by title or URL, or switch on AI search and ask in plain language
• Auto group: let AI sort your tabs into sensible groups by project and topic
• Suggest closes: let AI point out duplicates and stale tabs you can safely close
• Save and restore sessions, with automatic crash recovery snapshots

The AI features are optional. You bring your own key (Anthropic or OpenRouter), it stays on your device, and tab data is only sent when you click an AI button. Everything else works fully offline and stays local.
