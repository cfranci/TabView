# TabView Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-16_

TabView is a visual tab manager for Chrome. This policy explains what data it
touches and where that data goes. The short version: your data stays on your
device, except for optional AI requests that you trigger yourself.

## What TabView accesses

- **Tab information** (titles, URLs, favicons) for your open tabs, used to build
  the visual grid, search, grouping, and session features.
- **Tab screenshots and memory readings**, captured locally to show preview cards
  and per tab memory use.
- **Your settings, saved sessions, and crash recovery snapshots.**
- **An API key you optionally provide** for AI features.

## Where your data goes

- **Stays on your device by default.** Tab data, screenshots, settings, sessions,
  and your API key are stored locally in Chrome storage on your computer. TabView
  does not run any server and does not send your data to us. We never receive it.
- **Optional AI features.** If you enable AI (auto group, suggest closes, tab
  summaries, or natural language search) you supply your own API key for Anthropic
  or OpenRouter. Only when you click an AI action does TabView send the relevant
  tab titles and URLs to the provider you selected, solely to produce that result.
  Your key is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Those providers handle that
  data under their own privacy policies (Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy,
  OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy).
- If you do not enable AI features, no data leaves your device at all.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your data.
- We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.
- We do not track you, and we operate no backend that collects your information.
